# Pony Pals: My Little Stable

A gentle pony care game for young children (about 6), made to play in the browser on an iPad or an Android phone.

- Pick a pony colour and a name (picture choices, or a grown-up can type one).
- Feed (drag an apple, carrot or hay to the pony), brush off the mud and pick out the hooves, go for a ride and jump logs, then tuck the pony in at bedtime.
- Earn horseshoes for care and spend them on manes, a bow, a saddle and stable decorations. A foal and a bunny move in after a few days of care.
- No reading needed: everything is pictures, with spoken words (Web Speech API, en-GB) and simple word labels for reading practice.
- No ads, no purchases, no links, no accounts. Progress saves on the device (localStorage).
- Grown-ups: hold the padlock for 3 seconds for a play timer, sound settings, renaming and reset.

Static site, no build step. Serve the folder as-is (it is hosted on Render as a static site).
